## Summary

- Traffic throughput dropped significantly compared to 2.6.0: HTTP requests/sec fell by roughly 70%, and HTTPS requests/sec fell by about half. Average and max latency also increased across both protocols.
- Non-2xx or 3xx responses increased slightly compared to 2.6.0 for both HTTP and HTTPS, though they still represent a very small percentage of total requests.
- Unlike 2.6.0 (which reported no nginx-gateway errors), 2.7.0 shows a number of nginx-gateway errors related to leader election: "leader election lost", lease-lock "forbidden" (RBAC) errors, and lease-retrieval timeouts, recurring at multiple points across the 3-day test run. Could be due to cluster upgrades, but unknown.  Likely transient.
- NGINX reported a number of "no live upstreams" errors for /coffee and /tea, consistent with the pattern seen in 2.6.0, likely due to backend pod churn.
- Memory improved for NGINX, but was worse for NGF
